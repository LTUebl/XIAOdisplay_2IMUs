Knee Sleeve Sensor Placement & iPhone Direct Bluetooth Guide

1. Direct iPhone Bluetooth Connection (BLE)

To connect directly from an iPhone or iPad over Bluetooth Low Energy: 

1. Install the free "Bluefy – Web BLE Browser" app from the iOS App Store.
2. Power on the Knee Sleeve XIAO module (3.7V LiPo switch ON).
3. Open this web app URL in Bluefy and tap "Connect Bluetooth (iPhone / BLE)".
4. Select "XIAO-IMU" from the Bluetooth popup list.
5. Have the subject stand upright and still, then tap:
   "Start Recording (Auto-Calibrates Standing 0°)"
