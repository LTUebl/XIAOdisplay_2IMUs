📱 iPhone Direct Bluetooth & Knee Sleeve Guide

  1. Install Bluefy on iPhone: Download the free “Bluefy – Web BLE Browser” from the iOS App Store (Apple Safari blocks Web Bluetooth).
  2. Power On Knee Sleeve: Slide the Seeed XIAO 1.14" Display LiPo battery switch ON. Wait for the green/blue LED.
  3. Connect Bluetooth: Tap Connect Bluetooth (XIAO-IMU) at the top of this screen and pick XIAO-IMU.
  4. Stand Still & Tap Start Recording: Have the wearer stand upright in neutral posture (`0°`) for ~0.2 seconds while tapping Start Recording (Auto-Calibrates Standing 0°). The XIAO automatically zeroes gyroscope drift and calibrates the 3D standing reference pose right before recording starts!
  5. AirDrop / Save CSV: Tap Stop Recording, then tap Share / Export CSV to AirDrop or save your biomechanics dataset to iPhone Files.

🦿 Lateral Knee Sensor Mounting

  • XIAO 1.14" Display: Strapped to mid-thigh / distal anterior-lateral femur.
  • Sensor #1 (0x69, default AD0 open): Mounted on lateral distal femur (thigh cuff).
  • Sensor #2 (0x68, back AD0 jumper soldered): Mounted on lateral proximal tibia (shank cuff).
  • Axis Alignment: Sensor PCB flat face (`+Z`) points laterally outward along the knee flexion/extension hinge axis; STEMMA QT cable axis (`+X`) runs along the bone shaft.
